FINISHING THE GITHUB SETUP (your repository: georgefournier111/Life-168)
  1. Upload every file listed above to the top level of the repository
  2. Delete manifest.json and "manifest (1).json" from the repository
  3. In the repository, click Settings
  4. Click Pages in the left sidebar
  5. Source: "Deploy from a branch"
  6. Branch: main   Folder: / (root)   then click Save
  7. Wait about a minute. The address will be:
       https://georgefournier111.github.io/Life-168/
  8. Confirm it worked: visit
       https://georgefournier111.github.io/Life-168/sw.js
     You should see the service worker code, not a "404" page.

HOW PEOPLE INSTALL IT AS AN APP
  iPhone or iPad: open the address in Safari, tap Share, "Add to Home Screen"
  Android: open the address in Chrome, tap the three dot menu,
    "Add to Home screen"
  It opens full screen with the 168 icon, works offline, and each person's
  answers stay saved on their own device.

TO UPDATE THE APP LATER
  Replace index.html with the new version and change the cache name in
  sw.js from "wlll168-v4" to "wlll168-v5" (then v6, and so on) so
  installed copies pick up the update.

PRIVACY POLICY AND SUPPORT PAGES (required by Google Play)
  Two pages are included: privacy.html and support.html. The host serves them
  at clean addresses (no .html). Once your site is live, use these:
    https://app.life168.org/privacy
    https://app.life168.org/support
  Google Play requires a privacy policy URL. Paste the /privacy address
  into the Play Console store listing when asked. The app footer also links
  to both pages. (Use the clean addresses above, not the .html versions: the
  .html links redirect, which the installed app's service worker rejects.)

  IMPORTANT: both pages list support@life168.org as the contact email.
  Either set up that address (mail forwarding on your life168.org domain)
  so messages reach you, or change the two "support@life168.org" lines in
  privacy.html and support.html to an email you check.

THEN FOR GOOGLE PLAY

THE assetlinks.json STEP (do not skip this)
  The file at
    WLLL 168 App\.well-known\assetlinks.json
  is now REAL, not a template. It holds the actual upload key fingerprint
  and the correct package id. Verified 24 Sep 2026.

    package_name: org.life168.app.twa
    upload key  : 25:77:2B:6F:E4:D8:3A:0B:F7:49:9F:BA:99:87:0F:12:
                  CD:F1:C0:3A:B7:96:22:BF:CC:14:0A:9B:04:53:AA:5B

  As it stands it verifies a SIDELOADED apk (the one in Life 168 Google).
  It will NOT yet verify the app once Play installs it, because Play
  re-signs your upload with Google's own key. One more fingerprint has to
  be added after your first upload:

  1. Upload the .aab to Play Console.
  2. Go to: Play Console > Setup > App integrity > App signing
  3. Copy the SHA-256 under "App signing key certificate". This is
     GOOGLE'S key, not the one PWABuilder made.
  4. Add it as a SECOND entry in the sha256_cert_fingerprints array,
     keeping the upload key that is already there. Two entries, comma
     separated, each in quotes:

       "sha256_cert_fingerprints": [
         "25:77:2B:...:AA:5B",
         "PASTE GOOGLE'S APP SIGNING SHA-256 HERE"
       ]

  5. Re-upload the .well-known folder to the site root, then check:
       https://app.life168.org/.well-known/assetlinks.json
     It must return the JSON, not a 404.

  THE KEYSTORE IS IRREPLACEABLE
  Life 168 Google\signing.keystore plus signing-key-info.txt are the only
  way to ever publish an update. signing-key-info.txt holds the password
  in plain text and currently sits in OneDrive, so copy the folder to
  offline storage as well.

  WATCH OUT: folders starting with a dot are hidden by Windows Explorer
